# NutriCook AI — Loom Walkthrough Script (90 seconds)

## Before you record

- Open https://nutricook-ai-kappa.vercel.app in Chrome
- Sign in with your account (or use demo mode — no sign-in needed)
- Have a photo of food/fridge ready on your phone or use the sample below
- Record at 1x speed, no need to narrate — use Loom's auto-captions

---

## Script

**[0:00–0:10] — Home screen**  
Open the app. Show the Home dashboard — energy ring, next meal card, water tracker.  
*Voiceover:* "This is NutriCook AI. It's a nutrition coaching tool I built for my clients at TAMUCC — powered by Claude."

**[0:10–0:25] — Fridge Scan**  
Tap the AI tab. Tap the camera icon (Scan Fridge). Select a photo of food or a fridge.  
Watch the ingredients populate as chips.  
*Voiceover:* "Snap a photo of your fridge. Claude Vision reads what's there and pulls out every ingredient."

**[0:25–0:50] — Recipe Generation**  
Select a goal preset (e.g. "Muscle Gain"). Toggle a dietary pref if you want.  
Tap Generate. Watch three recipe cards stream in one by one — show the skeleton card updating with the name as Claude writes it.  
*Voiceover:* "Hit generate. Three personalized recipes stream in live — with full macros, ingredients, and step-by-step instructions."

**[0:50–1:05] — Save + Grocery**  
Tap Save on a recipe. Tap the Grocery tab. Show the ingredients from that recipe now in the list, grouped by aisle.  
*Voiceover:* "Save a recipe and its ingredients go straight to your grocery list, sorted by aisle."

**[1:05–1:20] — Profile / Streak**  
Tap Profile. Show the streak counter, weight chart, and macro split.  
*Voiceover:* "Everything syncs to your account — streaks, weight history, saved recipes. It learns your routine over time."

**[1:20–1:30] — Close**  
Return to Home.  
*Voiceover:* "Built for the Claude Corps Fellowship — because the best AI tools solve real problems for real people."
